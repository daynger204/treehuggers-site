---
question: "What are the potential human health effects associated with ground application of Btk?"
order: 6
---

Btk has been shown through scientific studies to have very low acute toxicity. If Btk gets on the skin, it may cause skin redness, rashes or irritation, but the likelihood is low. Btk can cause moderate eye irritation. If it gets into the eyes, flush with clean water for 20 minutes.
