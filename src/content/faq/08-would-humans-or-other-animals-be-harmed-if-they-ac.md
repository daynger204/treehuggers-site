---
question: "Would humans or other animals be harmed if they accidentally ingested Btk?"
order: 8
---

Humans and other mammals have highly acidic environments in their stomachs that destroy Btk before it can cause infection. Because the Btk endotoxin requires extremely alkaline conditions to become active (such as those found in the guts of gypsy moths and other caterpillars). Btk does not affect animals with acidic stomach environments such as birds, fish, and mammals.
